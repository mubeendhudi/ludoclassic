How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its yard.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent or reaching home gives an extra roll.
  • Three 6s in a row cancel your turn.
  • Landing on an opponent sends their token back to the yard, except on safe squares (★ and start squares).
  • Reach the centre with an exact roll. First player to bring all 4 tokens home wins.
  • Tap the dice (or press Space) to roll, then tap a glowing token.
